About Us

For the past 20 years, Amaravathi Travels has grown from a single-vehicle operation into a trusted leader in local and long-distance transport. Starting two decades ago with just one car, our dedication to reliability, safety, and customer satisfaction enabled us to stand where we are today.

Over the last 20 years, we have safely transported thousands of passengers, expanded our service routes across the region, and continuously upgraded our vehicles with modern amenities to ensure a comfortable journey every step of the way.
